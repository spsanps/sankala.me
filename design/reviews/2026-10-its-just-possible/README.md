# "It's just possible": review

## Art pass (October 3, 2026)

San's notes after reading milestone 1 live: the essay must follow the site's taste rules (its own
art style, art and page as one world), and "my messages, prompts and system prompts should be
highlighted better". The text is his rewrite from main, unchanged.

- **Message cards.** Each blockquote of `**Agent:**` / `**Me:**` lines is a small seatback screen:
  sender label, a running message number (MSG 01 to 08), my lines in amber and set to the right,
  the agent's in cyan. Real text in a `<blockquote>`, white or near-white on navy (above 11:1).
- **Prompt and note cards.** The opening prompt, the standing goal and the standing notes read as
  what I typed: a system-prompt card in JetBrains Mono with a caret, labelled Prompt, Standing goal
  and Standing notes. The labels come from the paragraph that introduces each block.
- **The figure**, "The last 72 hours, as a flight", closes the section "The weekend": a seatback
  screen with the live map, a panel (time, both ranks and scores, GPUs online, time to deadline),
  the current waypoint's messages, and a flight-progress scrubber with Play, previous and next.
  It opens on Sunday 18:10, both tracks first. Arrow keys move 30 minutes, Page Up and Page Down
  six hours, Home and End to either end. With reduced motion, Play steps from waypoint to waypoint.
  The map's still is in the HTML; the canvas is drawn over it when the figure nears the screen.
  "The 72 hours as text" lists every event; the Markdown mirror and crawler edition carry it too.
- **Cover.** A tighter fit, a cased and glowing route, a larger title plate. The live layer: the
  plane inches along, the dots ahead march to Pittsburgh and the rank readouts flicker now and then.
- **Section strips.** The essay opens with "Now boarding · 8 min to destination", each section
  with the plane as far along as the reader is and the reading time left, and lands at "Arrived".
