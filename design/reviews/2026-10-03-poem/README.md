**Style: sgraffito on red earthenware** (new in the style register). The figure is a cup on a
banding wheel. Its decoration is white slip scratched through to the red clay, and it is the
habitat, wrapped around the cup: the lamp, the house with POTTERY on the roof, fields
climbing, the lake, the woods, the orchard and the train, with the far shore's lamps under the
lip. The wheel turns once a minute. The drawing simulates slip thickness, burrs and crumbs
along the cuts, throwing lines, a dip line held by three fingertips, and a handle that comes
round. The cover is a brushed slab in the same hand: the habitat seen end-on as a ring, the
house at the bottom, the orchard overhead and a cup in the middle.

**How the stanzas were read:** San's file has no blank lines. Each line that starts without a
space begins a stanza; lines that start with a space continue it. That gives 32 stanzas and 110
lines. On the page each stanza opens at the margin and its other lines are indented a little
(1.2em). On phones, a line too long for the screen wraps deeper than that, so a wrap never looks
like a new line. The dialogue is set as the file has it: "You made this?", Yes. and "What do I
owe you?" are one-line stanzas, and Nothing. opens the stanza that ends with "Nobody owes
anything now." Straight quotes and apostrophes became curly. No other character was changed.
