The chatbot won’t stay in the chat window.

I’ve started turning my essays into animated films. The first one is GPT-7 Will Have Arms.

The argument is that the model you talk to and the model running a robot may end up being the same kind of thing. We tend to imagine one AI with one body. But software doesn’t have to work that way. One model could operate many bodies.

That is the part I keep coming back to. If the same improvement can reach lots of robots, progress in the physical world could look different from progress in a single lab.

There are still hard questions here: how much learning transfers between bodies, how reliable the control is, and when a robot needs to make decisions locally. The essay makes a stronger bet on convergence. “GPT-7” is my shorthand for that future, not a claim about an announced model.

A place to put the next ones

This letter will collect the essays, films, and experiments as I make them. I’m interested in AI and robotics, but also in what we build with them—and what it would be like to live there.

For that last part, there’s Another Sky: a space habitat you can walk around inside. Look up.

Which part of the robotics argument seems least likely to you? I’d like to hear it.
