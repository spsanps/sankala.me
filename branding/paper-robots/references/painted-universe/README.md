## Application to channel identity

Banner version 7 combines the sunflower opening from the GPT-7 essay with this
world's robot, passages and characters. DP version 3 repaints the established
paper robot in the same medium. The old head-outline diagram, mismatched
photo-like opening robot and empty desktop ends were specifically rejected.
The O'Neill cylinder and generic robot city were also rejected earlier.

Use these as visual references, not factual documentation of an AI incident.
The supplied storyboards include dramatic events and company imagery; their
claims were not researched as part of this artwork task. The channel assets
omit their captions and company logos.
