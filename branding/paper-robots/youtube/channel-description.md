Animated essays about AI, robots, and possible futures.

Paper Robots explores intelligence, technology, and the worlds we could build—from robots in everyday life to habitats in space. The aim is to make difficult ideas understandable and think carefully about what would make the future go well.

A recurring focus is AI safety and alignment: making sure increasingly powerful AI systems serve human values and remain under human control.

Created by San Kala, an AI researcher and builder.
